How it works

Online forms often reject photos that are too big. SnapKB lowers the JPG quality step by step until your photo fits the size you choose, and only shrinks the dimensions if it has to. Everything runs in your browser, so it works even on slow connections once the page has loaded.

Questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The compression runs inside your browser, so your image never leaves your device.

Why does my photo look smaller?

If quality alone cannot reach the target size, the tool also shrinks the width and height a little. Pick a larger KB value for a sharper result.

Which formats work?

You can open JPG, PNG, WEBP and most other image types. The result is saved as JPG (best for forms) or WEBP.
