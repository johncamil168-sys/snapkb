About SnapKB

A small, free tool for people stuck on photo size limits.

SnapKB was built to solve one annoying problem: online forms rejecting photos because they are too big. It compresses images to an exact size in KB, right in your browser, without uploading anything.

The site is kept free by small ads and by optional printable products. Questions or ideas? Contact the owner through the shop page.
